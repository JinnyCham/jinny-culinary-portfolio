my story

my story

My journey with food began early. I started making bread at 15 — drawn to the patience, the detail, and the quiet discipline behind something so simple and so unforgiving.

That early curiosity grew into a genuine commitment. Over time I moved toward professional kitchens, pastry, and the wider world of fine dining and luxury hospitality. Each environment pushed me further and asked more of me.

The environments I value most are those where standards are high, teamwork matters, and growth is earned through consistency — not just talent.
