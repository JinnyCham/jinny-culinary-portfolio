About Me

About Me

I chose culinary because I wanted to build something real with my hands, my discipline, and my energy. What began as interest has become genuine commitment.

I enjoy environments where standards are high, details matter, and every service demands focus. Pressure does not discourage me. It sharpens me. In fast-paced kitchens, I often feel more alive, more focused, and more determined to do things well.

I know I am still early in my career, but I take growth seriously. I actively look for opportunities that challenge me, teach me, and help me become stronger both technically and mentally.

I am not looking for comfort. I am looking for growth.

Selected Work

Selected Work

A few dishes and creations that reflect what I care about most: flavour, detail, balance, and presentation.

01

Spotted Grouper

Asian spice glaze · coconut katsuobushi sauce · roe

Balancing the bold spice glaze with the delicacy of the fish required careful timing and restraint. Every element on the plate had a purpose.

02

Vanilla Cauliflower Velouté

Vadouvan foam · citrus · chestnut

A warm, composed dish built around subtle contrast — the sweetness of vanilla against spiced vadouvan, finished with bright citrus and earthy chestnut.

03

Oyster Aspic

Floral garnish · Sichuan pepper vinaigrette

A cold preparation where precision in setting, garnish placement, and seasoning were everything. The Sichuan pepper vinaigrette added a quiet, lingering heat.

04

Araguani Raspberry Éclair

Raspberry chantilly · Araguani dark chocolate · French meringue

Finding the right balance between raspberry's natural acidity and the depth of Araguani chocolate. The meringue introduced texture and a softer sweetness.

05

Dulcey Citrus Tart

Dulcey chocolate mousse · citrus compote · pâte sucrée · jaconde base

Dulcey's caramel warmth needed to stay present without overwhelming the citrus. Getting the glaze level and the shard placement right took several attempts.

06

Shio Pan

Japanese salted bread · enriched dough · handmade from scratch

Simple on the surface. Everything underneath depends on how well you handle the dough, how you time the lamination, and how precisely you fold the butter.
